4. Table 6 numbers and mu_1. The June parameters for the S-shaped and hooked models were not actually at AUC 0.90. The S-shaped model (mu_1 = 2.4, sigma_1 = 1.4) sat at AUC 0.918, and the hooked model (means 2.5 and 0.8) at 0.887. On July 31, when we replaced the threshold-grid search with an exact root-find, we also recalibrated all three models to AUC 0.90 exactly, which is why mu_1 became 2.205 and the hooked means became 2.716 and 0.869. The S-shaped model lost some area, so its monthly allowance fell from 145 to 72 days; the hooked model gained area, so its allowance rose from 66 to 88. The equal-variance model was already exact and barely moved. You are right that this should have been explained at the time. The exact solver also shifts other entries by up to about a day (for example 182 became 183.2 for monthly seizures at AUC 0.90).

   The vertical segment at the far right of Figure S5: that is the S-shaped (blue) curve, and it was in the June figure as well. With sigma_1 = 1.4 > 1, seizure-day scores are more spread out than non-seizure scores, so at very low scores seizure days actually outnumber non-seizure days. As days in warning approach 365, the handful of days still forecast safe are mostly seizure days, and the risk on those days shoots up. It only happens in the last fraction of a day. It is the same tail effect that prevents the S-shaped model from reaching the threshold for weekly seizures. I added one sentence in Section 4.3 saying so.

5. The weekly-seizure sentence in 4.1. The sentence was technically true (155 days is not 365) but it reads as "cannot drive." It was in the June version too. It now gives the range: weekly-seizure patients remain heavily restricted at AUC 0.90, 20 to 155 driving days per year across the q_f range.

The paragraph is replaced by three sentences saying that the S-shaped failure is a property of the unequal-variance model: the heavier lower tail on seizure-day scores puts a floor on the residual seizure probability (0.0089 for weekly seizures) that sits above the ceiling (0.0025), so no threshold works. Developer recommendation 3, which repeated the same idea, is deleted.
